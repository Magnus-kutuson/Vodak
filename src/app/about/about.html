I'm Nana Dumfeh Agyeman-Badu, a results-driven Graphic and UI/UX Designer based in Accra, Ghana. With over 5 years of hands-on experience, I create compelling visual identities, intuitive app interfaces, and high-impact marketing materials.

I hold a BSc in Landscape Design and Management from KNUST, which gives me a unique eye for spatial thinking and environmental aesthetics that I bring to every digital canvas.

Proven ability to lead cross-functional design teams, deliver projects on time, and drive measurable business outcomes — adept at translating complex requirements into elegant, user-centred designs.
